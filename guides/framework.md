# Social Life Framework

A structured approach to building a social life you actually want: friends you see, a community you belong to, a network that knows what you do, and — if you want one — a romantic partner. These are the same skills pointed at different goals. Noticing who is open to contact, starting a conversation, following up, and turning a stranger into someone you know work the same way at a climbing gym, a conference, or a first date.

It is written to be adaptable across genders, orientations, ages and goals. Throughout, consent, honesty, respect and safety are non-negotiable, and the aim is real connection rather than a numbers game.

New here? Start with the [Quick-Start Guide](quick-start-guide.md).

## How to use this framework

The guides roughly follow the arc of meeting someone, but they aren't strictly linear:

- **Foundation first.** Ground yourself in Personal Development, Safety and Vetting, and Boundaries and Consent before you invest in new people — but you don't need to be "finished" to start.
- **Then the shared arc.** Choose where to invest (Venues), read who is open (Offline Signals), open and keep a conversation going (Opening the Conversation, Strategies), and turn one encounter into a second (Direct Contact).
- **Then pick your tracks.** Friendship, community, professional network and romance each have their own guides. Work one or several; most people find that progress in one spills into the others.
- **Throughout.** Protect your energy with Resilience, and recalibrate with Evaluation and Reflection. Record what actually happens in the [journal](../journal/index.md), the people you want to stay in touch with in [people](../people/index.md), and the places that work in [venues](../venues/index.md).
