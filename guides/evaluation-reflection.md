# Evaluation and Reflection

Systems for tracking progress and learning from experience. Reflection is most useful when it drives learning rather than self-judgment — hold it alongside [Resilience and Handling Setbacks](resilience-setbacks.md) so review builds you up instead of grinding you down. Note that metrics below are prompts for insight, not a scoreboard; quality of connection matters more than conversion rates.

## Regular Check-ins

- Weekly reflection on interactions and conversations
- Monthly assessment of venue effectiveness
- Quarterly review of strategy adjustments
- Annual evaluation of relationship patterns

## Signals to Track

- Number of new introductions per month
- How often initial meetings lead to a genuine second connection
- Average relationship duration and reasons for ending
- Venues where you meet the most compatible people
- Common patterns in successful vs. unsuccessful connections
- One pattern worth tracking specifically: does your interest survive reciprocation, or collapse precisely when someone shows interest in you? That timing — interest withdrawn as a function of being chosen — is the signature of [Fear of Success and Pre-emptive Self-Sabotage](fear-of-success-intimacy.md), and it's easy to misread as "just bad luck" until you map it across several encounters.

## Learning Questions

- What types of people are you drawn to? Why?
- Which venues feel most natural and productive?
- What are your biggest conversation blockers? (map them with [Inner Blocks and Light Social Anxiety](blocks-social-anxiety.md))
- When do you feel most confident and authentic?
- What patterns repeat across different relationships?

## Feedback Sources

- Close friends and their honest observations
- Therapy or coaching if pursuing deeper work
- Your own gut feelings and intuition
- Post-relationship reflections and growth areas
- Comparing your intentions vs. actual behavior

## Adjustment Framework

- Identify what's working and double down
- Recognize what's draining and minimize
- Experiment with new venues or approaches
- Adjust expectations based on real feedback
- Pivot strategy when current approach isn't yielding results
