# Flirting Techniques

Building attraction and rapport through playful, confident interaction. Flirting works only when it reads and respects the other person's signals — the "reading signals" and consent thread here connects directly to [Boundaries and Consent](boundaries-consent.md).

## Body Language

- Open posture and relaxed shoulders as a baseline signal
- Eye contact: holding it a beat longer than usual, then breaking naturally
- Mirroring the other person's energy and gestures
- Leaning in slightly during engaging moments
- Genuine smiling versus performative smiling

## Verbal Flirting

- Teasing and light banter over flattery
- Playful challenges ("prove it," mock disagreement)
- Callbacks to earlier jokes or shared moments
- Compliments that are specific and earned, not generic
- Using a warmer tone of voice and varied pacing

## Reading Signals

- Reciprocated eye contact and prolonged engagement
- Mirroring back your body language or energy
- Leaning in, angling their body toward you
- Laughing at things that aren't objectively that funny
- Finding reasons to prolong the interaction

For the signals that come *before* an interaction — the offline equivalent of swiping right or left — see [Offline Signals: The Body-Language Equivalent of Swiping](nonverbal-signals.md).

## Building Tension

- Comfortable pauses instead of filling every silence
- Push-pull: warmth followed by playful withdrawal
- Withholding full attention occasionally to create curiosity
- Light, appropriate physical touch (hand, arm) as a test — read consent carefully
- Letting attraction build gradually rather than rushing it

## Common Mistakes

- Over-complimenting or trying too hard to impress
- Ignoring disinterest signals and pushing anyway
- Relying on flirting to substitute for genuine conversation
- Being overly sarcastic without warmth underneath
- Losing playfulness by getting too serious too fast
