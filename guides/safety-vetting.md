# Safety and Vetting

Protecting your physical and emotional safety while meeting and dating new people.

## Before Meeting In Person

- Video or voice call before a first in-person meeting to confirm identity
- Reverse image search photos if something feels off
- Notice inconsistencies between their profile, texts, and calls
- Be wary of anyone who refuses to talk on the phone or video
- Trust hesitation — you never owe anyone a meeting

## First Meeting Precautions

- Meet in a public, well-populated place, never a home or isolated spot
- Arrange your own transportation both ways; don't get picked up
- Tell a friend where you're going, who with, and when to expect a check-in
- Share your live location with someone you trust
- Keep your phone charged and your first drink in view

## Vetting Over Time

- Verify claims that matter (employment, marital status) as trust builds
- Notice whether their story stays consistent across weeks
- Meet their friends and see them in their own social context
- Be cautious about sharing your home address early
- Watch how they respond to a "no" — a safe person accepts it easily

## Warning Signs of Predatory Behavior

- Rushing intimacy, commitment, or "I've never felt this way" too fast (love bombing)
- Pushing your boundaries after you've stated them
- Isolating you from friends or family
- Pressuring you to keep the relationship secret
- Requests for money, gifts, or financial help — a near-universal scam signal
- Anger or guilt-tripping when you set limits

## Digital and Financial Safety

- Never send money, gift cards, or crypto to someone you haven't met
- Keep financial accounts, passwords, and IDs private until deep trust exists
- Be careful sharing your full name, workplace, or home before you're ready
- Use the dating app's chat until you've built real comfort
- Report and block anyone who violates boundaries or asks for money

Safety underpins everything else. When in doubt, see [Compatibility Assessment and Red Flags](compatibility-red-flags.md) for how these signals compound over time, and [Boundaries and Consent](boundaries-consent.md) for enforcing limits.
