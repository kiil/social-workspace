# Digital Communication and Online Dating

Navigating apps, texting, and the online-to-offline transition that defines modern dating.

## Profile and Presence

- Photos that are recent, clear, and show your face and full body
- A mix of solo shots, a genuine smile, and one activity/hobby photo
- Avoiding group-only photos, heavy filters, and mirror selfies
- A bio with specifics and hooks, not clichés ("love to laugh," "fluent in sarcasm")
- Giving people concrete things to ask you about

## First Messages

- Referencing something specific from their profile, not "hey"
- Asking an open question that's easy and fun to answer
- Matching effort — a short opener invites a short reply
- Keeping it light; save depth for when there's momentum
- Not sending a paragraph or a compliment on appearance alone

## Texting Cadence

- Matching their pace rather than over- or under-investing
- Avoiding double- and triple-texting when there's no reply
- Not reading into response times; people are busy
- Keeping banter playful and forward-moving toward a plan
- Recognizing when texting has replaced actually meeting

## Moving Off the App

- Proposing to meet within a week or so of good conversation
- Not letting a match go stale in endless small talk (pen-pal trap)
- Suggesting a voice or video call as a low-stakes bridge
- Reading reluctance to meet as meaningful information
- Keeping early plans short, public, and low-pressure

## App-Specific Dynamics

- Swipe-based apps favor volume; expect noise and low reply rates
- Interest- and prompt-based apps reward specificity and effort
- Rotating a couple of platforms beats grinding one
- Refreshing photos and prompts when a profile goes stale
- Managing multiple conversations without losing authenticity

## Avoiding Digital Pitfalls

- Guarding against burnout from endless swiping (see [Resilience and Handling Setbacks](resilience-setbacks.md))
- Not outsourcing your entire dating life to algorithms
- Keeping identifying and financial details private early (see [Safety and Vetting](safety-vetting.md))
- Not mistaking a good texter for a good match
- Treating the app as a way to meet, not a relationship in itself
