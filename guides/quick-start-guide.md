# Quick-Start Guide

A practical entry point — where to begin, how the pieces fit, and the rhythm to work them in.

## Who this is for

- Anyone who wants more people in their life: friends, a community, a professional network, a partner, or all four
- Adaptable across genders, orientations, ages and relationship goals — adjust specifics to your context
- Assumes you want real connection, not manipulation or a numbers game
- Non-negotiables throughout: consent, honesty, respect and safety

## Start here (foundation first)

- Begin with [Personal Development](personal-development.md) — self-awareness and stability make everything else work
- Read [Safety and Vetting](safety-vetting.md) and [Boundaries and Consent](boundaries-consent.md) before meeting anyone new one-on-one
- You don't need to be "finished" — growth and a social life happen in parallel
- If you already know you freeze before approaching, add [Inner Blocks and Light Social Anxiety](blocks-social-anxiety.md). If, in romance, you pull away *after* someone shows interest, add [Fear of Success and Pre-emptive Self-Sabotage](fear-of-success-intimacy.md). Skill work won't move either one on its own.

## The shared arc

- Decide where to invest: [Venues and Contexts](venues-contexts.md)
- Read who is open to contact: [Offline Signals](nonverbal-signals.md)
- Start and carry a conversation: [Opening the Conversation](opening-conversation.md) and [Strategies and Tactics](strategies-tactics.md)
- Turn an encounter into a second one: [Direct Contact Initiation and Assertiveness](direct-contact-initiation.md)

## Building the habit

- After an interaction worth remembering: write a short entry in the [journal](../journal/index.md) and, if you want to see them again, a note in [people](../people/index.md) with a next step
- Weekly: look at the people whose next step is due, and reflect on what felt authentic
- Monthly: review which venues and approaches are working
- Continuously: protect your energy with [Resilience and Handling Setbacks](resilience-setbacks.md)
- Periodically: recalibrate with [Evaluation and Reflection](evaluation-reflection.md)

## A few principles

- Quality of connection over quantity of contacts
- A no is fit information, not a verdict on you
- Your boundaries and safety are never up for negotiation
- Most connections are made in the follow-up, not the first meeting
- Have a life full enough that other people add to it rather than define it
