# Strategies and Tactics

Practical approaches for initiating contact and building rapport — with a potential friend, collaborator or partner alike. For apps, texting, and the online-to-offline transition specifically, see the dedicated [Digital Communication and Online Dating](digital-communication.md) guide; this document covers the in-person and cross-cutting fundamentals.

## Approach and Initiation

- Opening lines that reference shared context or observation (worked through in [Opening the Conversation After a Green Light](opening-conversation.md))
- Reading receptiveness before approaching (see [Offline Signals: The Body-Language Equivalent of Swiping](nonverbal-signals.md))
- Timing: choosing low-pressure moments to engage
- Group vs. one-on-one approach dynamics
- Following up promptly after a good first interaction

## Conversation Tactics

- Balancing curiosity with sharing about yourself
- Escalating from small talk to genuine connection
- Humor and playfulness as tension-breakers
- Active listening and building on what they say
- Knowing when to end on a high note

## Escalation and Pacing

- Matching pace to mutual interest and comfort
- Physical escalation cues and consent (see [Boundaries and Consent](boundaries-consent.md))
- Defining the relationship at the right moment
- Balancing pursuit with giving space
- Recognizing when to slow down or pull back

## Handling Setbacks

- Graceful responses to rejection or disinterest
- Not taking lack of response personally
- Learning from interactions that fizzled
- Maintaining momentum across multiple prospects
- Avoiding scarcity mindset and desperation signals

For the deeper emotional side of rejection, social or dating fatigue, and knowing when to take a break, see [Resilience and Handling Setbacks](resilience-setbacks.md).
