# 2026-11-10

## What happened

Saw Sam at the museum café, the person from the photography talk last month. Made eye contact, nodded, didn't go over. Sam left ten minutes later.

## What I noticed

The block was the usual one: "they probably don't remember me." There was no evidence for that — the nod came back. See [Inner Blocks and Light Social Anxiety](../guides/blocks-social-anxiety.md).

## Next time

Say hello and use the context: "Weren't you at the photography talk?" Added a next step to [Sam's note](../people/sam-rivera.md).
